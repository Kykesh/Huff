# Workflow — Kids (AI Animated) Niche

The highest-risk, highest-watch-time niche. Lock in on the warnings before reading the workflow.

## ⚠️ The risk warnings (read before anything else)

1. **YouTube Kids policy is strict.** Many AI-generated kids channels get demonetized or banned within months. Build with this expected.
2. **COPPA compliance is non-negotiable.** You CANNOT use behavioral ads. You must mark every video as "Made for Kids." Comments will be disabled. End screens limited.
3. **The "$23K/mo" stories are survivor bias.** For every winner, dozens of channels fail.
4. **Quality matters more than people admit.** Toddlers watch endlessly, but parents control the device — if your content looks shoddy, they swipe away.
5. **The platform may crack down further.** YouTube has tightened rules every 12-18 months on AI kids content. Plan to pivot.

If those don't deter you, here's the workflow.

## The "reverse-engineer + remix" pipeline

This is the approach documented by the $500/day operator and the $23K/mo operator:

### Step 1 — Find a winner
Open YouTube Kids. Search broad terms ("nursery rhymes", "vehicles for toddlers", "shapes song"). Find a video with millions of views from the last 30 days.

### Step 2 — Extract the formula
Copy the description word-for-word. Paste into Claude with this prompt:

```
This YouTube Kids video description is from a video with millions of views:

[PASTE DESCRIPTION]

1. What is the core psychological trigger that makes toddlers watch this 5+ times?
2. What is the visual style?
3. What is the music/audio pattern?
4. What characters or objects are central?
5. What's the structure (intro / loop / payoff)?

Now: build a COMPLETELY FRESH creative concept that triggers the same psychological response but with:
- Different characters
- Different scene
- Different color palette
- Different specific actions

Output a full creative brief I can hand to an AI video generator.
```

### Step 3 — Generate the video
Take Claude's output. Paste into Kling 3.0 (or Runway, or Pika). One prompt, one click. Generated video typically takes 5-15 minutes to render.

### Step 4 — Add music
Use Suno to generate the soundtrack or nursery rhyme. Example Suno prompt:
```
Cheerful nursery rhyme, kids music, vocal melody for toddlers,
ukulele and xylophone, bright energy, repetitive simple lyrics about
[topic — e.g. counting cars], 60 seconds, kid-friendly, no minor keys.
```

### Step 5 — Assemble in CapCut
- Drop video into timeline
- Drop music into audio track
- Cut to 1-3 minute length (kids attention span)
- Loop visually if needed (toddlers love loops)
- Add simple text overlays if it's educational (counting, letters)
- Export 1920x1080 or 1080x1080

### Step 6 — Thumbnail
The thumbnail needs to appeal to PARENTS, not kids (parents pick the video):
- Bright primary colors
- One clear focal character
- 2-4 words: "Counting Trucks!" / "ABC Song"
- Smiling, friendly expression

### Step 7 — Upload
- Title: simple, search-friendly ("Counting Trucks for Toddlers | Learn Numbers 1-10")
- Description: 100 words, keyword-rich
- Tags: nursery rhyme, kids song, [character], [topic]
- ⚠️ **MUST mark "Made for Kids"** — failure to do so = channel terminated

## Content formats that work

1. **Counting / ABC videos** — evergreen demand
2. **Vehicle videos** — trucks, trains, planes (huge for toddler boys especially)
3. **Animal song videos** — dancing animals + simple melody
4. **Color learning** — characters changing colors
5. **Sleep / lullaby content** — long-form (8-12 hours) — high watch time, low RPM but compounds

## Posting frequency

Aggressive: 1 video per day
Sustainable: 3 per week
Minimum: 2 per week

Volume matters here MORE than other niches because the algorithm rewards "frequent fresh kids content."

## The honest scenario planning

**Best case (top 10%):** $20K+/month within a year.

**Median case:** Channel never monetizes, gets demonetized, or stays under $500/month.

**Bottom case:** Channel banned for COPPA violation or "low-quality AI content."

Treat this as a high-variance bet. Don't quit your day job for it.

## Exit ramp

If/when the kids niche tightens further, the same workflow (Kling 3.0 + Suno + ElevenLabs) pivots to:
- General animated story channels
- ASMR / sleep content
- Lofi study channels (anime-style, no COPPA risk)

The Suno music alone can be sold on Musicbed/Artlist for $19-$199 per pack. The Kling video skills transfer to TikTok/IG/short-form.

Don't put all of this in one basket.
