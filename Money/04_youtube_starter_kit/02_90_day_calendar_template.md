# 90-Day Content Calendar Template

The goal of the first 90 days is not to go viral. It's to ship enough videos that the algorithm has data to work with (20–30 videos minimum). Then double down on what works.

## The prompt to generate your 12-video roadmap

```
Build a 90-day YouTube content calendar for my faceless channel.
Niche: [YOUR NICHE]
Channel identity: [PASTE FROM 01_channel_identity_template.md]
Content pillars: [PASTE 4 PILLARS]

Output:

1. 12 VIDEO TITLES
   - Each title must include a specific number and a curiosity hook
   - 3 titles per pillar
   - Each title under 60 characters
   - Mix: 6 SEO-focused (high search volume), 6 virality-focused (high CTR potential)

2. UPLOAD SCHEDULE
   - 3 videos per week for 4 weeks (12 videos)
   - Specific days of the week and times (based on niche)
   - Which videos to prioritize for SEO (consistent search demand) vs. virality (browse traffic)

3. CONTENT PROGRESSION
   - Which 4 videos build channel authority (cornerstone content)
   - Which 4 videos test new formats
   - Which 4 videos chase trends in the niche

4. THUMBNAIL CONCEPTS
   - For each title, one thumbnail concept (visual + 3-5 word text overlay)

5. CROSS-VIDEO BINGE LOOPS
   - For each video, which OTHER video in the 12 it should link to in end screen
   - Why (the curiosity bridge between the two)
```

## The execution rule

Once the calendar is set, don't deviate for 30 days. Resist the urge to swap in "better" topics. Even if video 3 flops, do video 4 as planned. The algorithm needs data, not your best guesses.

After 30 days (videos 1-12 done):
- Identify the top 3 performers (by views OR by retention rate)
- Find what they share — title structure, topic, format
- Build the next 12 videos around that pattern

This is how you go from random ideas to a content machine.
