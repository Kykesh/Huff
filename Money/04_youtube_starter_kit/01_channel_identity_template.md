# Channel Identity Template

Fill this in BEFORE producing a single video. Use the prompt below in Claude to do it in 5 minutes.

## The prompt

```
I'm starting a faceless YouTube channel about [NICHE].
Target audience: [DESCRIBE]
My unique angle: [WHAT YOU KNOW OR CAN DO THAT MOST IN THIS NICHE CAN'T]

Create a full channel identity:

1. CHANNEL NAME — 5 options
   - Must be: searchable, memorable, niche-clear in <3 seconds
   - Avoid: numbers, weird spellings, anything trademark-adjacent

2. TAGLINE — one sentence under the channel name
   - Tells a stranger what they get from subscribing

3. AUDIENCE PERSONA — 1 paragraph
   - Demographics, mental state, what they Google, what frustrates them
   
4. CONTENT PILLARS — 4 pillars
   - Each is a category that produces 5+ video ideas
   - Together they cover the niche

5. UNIQUE MECHANISM
   - The "thing only this channel does" — the angle, format, or POV
   - Why does someone subscribe to THIS channel vs. the 50 other ones in this niche?

6. CHANNEL DESCRIPTION (50 words)
   - For YouTube's About page
   - Includes 3 main keywords

7. THUMBNAIL STYLE — 1 paragraph
   - Color palette (2 colors max)
   - Text style (font weight, color, size)
   - Composition principle

8. BANNER COPY — 1 sentence
   - For your channel banner
```
