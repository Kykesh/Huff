## The compliance layer (READ THIS FIRST)

Finance content has legal risk. Build these into every video:

1. **Disclaimer slide at start AND in description:** "This is for educational purposes only and is not financial advice. Consult a licensed financial advisor."
2. **Never** make specific buy/sell recommendations for individual viewers
3. **Always** disclose if you hold positions in stocks you discuss
4. **Use** phrases like "some investors consider..." instead of "you should buy..."
5. **Save** sources for every claim — YouTube may demand them during channel reviews

## Niche-specific format ideas

| Format | Why it works | Example title |
|--------|-------------|---------------|
| "X stocks for [year]" | Massive evergreen search | "5 Dividend Stocks for 2026" |
| "Why I sold [X]" | Personal stake | "Why I Sold $50K of [stock]" |
| "[Topic] crash explained" | Timely + emotional | "The [Sector] Crash: What Happened" |
| "[Demographic] should invest like this" | Specific audience | "How 30-Year-Olds Should Invest $10K" |
| "What if you invested $X in [year]" | Historical curiosity | "If You Put $1K in NVIDIA in 2010..." |

## The thumbnail formula

Finance thumbnails are red/green arrows + dollar signs + a face/object. Since you're faceless:
- Use a chart screenshot with annotations
- Big number ("+$50K" or "-32%")
- 2 colors: usually green/red on dark background
- 3-4 word text overlay

Examples that work:
- "$10K → $1M"
- "BUY THIS DROP"
- "$500/DAY INCOME"

## Metadata specifics

Finance has the highest competition for keywords. Your description should:
- First sentence with the primary keyword
- Long-tail keywords in the next 5 sentences
- Compliance disclaimer
- Affiliate links (brokerage referrals are massive — $50-$300 per signup)
- 15 tags optimized via TubeBuddy (use the Keyword Explorer)
