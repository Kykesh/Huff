## The fastest path to first video

### Day 1 (2 hours)
1. Read `00_niche_analysis.md` → pick a niche
2. Open `01_channel_identity_template.md` → fill it in with Claude's help
3. Open the workflow file for your niche → install the tools

### Day 2 (3 hours)
1. Open `02_90_day_calendar_template.md` → generate 12 video ideas using the prompt inside
2. Pick video #1 (highest SEO potential, lowest production difficulty)
3. Use `03_script_template.md` to write it with Claude

### Day 3 (3 hours)
1. Generate voiceover (ElevenLabs)
2. Assemble video (your niche's workflow)
3. Use `04_seo_metadata_template.md` for title/description/tags
4. Upload

You now have your first video shipped in 3 days.

## The 4 mistakes to NOT make

1. **Picking a niche based on what you love, not what pays.** Read the niche analysis. Pick based on RPM × competition × repeatability.
2. **Trying to ship video 1 in one day.** It takes 3–5 days for your first. Video 5 will take 2 days. Video 20 will take 4 hours.
3. **Skipping the channel identity step.** Without consistent branding, even good content looks amateur and gets buried.
4. **Quitting at video 8.** The algorithm catches on around videos 20–30. Every successful channel guide says this. Most ignore it.

## The 90-day expectation

| Week | What you do | What you'll see |
|------|-------------|-----------------|
| 1 | First video shipped | 50–200 views |
| 2–4 | 3 videos/wk, testing formats | 100–1K views per video |
| 5–8 | Double down on best format | First video to hit 5K+ |
| 9–12 | YPP eligibility approaching | One video hits 20K+ (the signal) |

If by week 8 you have NO video over 2K views: switch niches or your hook style is broken. Run the Channel Clone Engine prompt from `02_prompt_library/05_content_creation_prompts.md`.
