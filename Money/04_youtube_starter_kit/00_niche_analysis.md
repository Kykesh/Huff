# Niche Analysis — Pick One

This is the decision document. Read once. Pick a niche. Don't reread looking for permission to switch later — that's how channels die at video 8.

## The 4 niches in this kit

| Niche | RPM | Competition | Repeatability | Production complexity | Best for |
|-------|-----|-------------|---------------|----------------------|----------|
| AI / Tech / Productivity | $12–$30 | HIGH | 8/10 | Low (text + B-roll) | You're already in this ecosystem; want to leverage existing knowledge |
| Finance / Investing | $15–$50 | VERY HIGH | 7/10 | Medium (needs charts) | You have a strong finance angle nobody else has |
| Data / Rankings | $5–$15 | MEDIUM | 9/10 | High (Remotion setup) | You like systems; the $372K/mo model |
| Kids (AI animated) | $2–$8 | MEDIUM | 10/10 | High (Kling 3.0) | You can tolerate YouTube Kids policy risk |

## Scoring framework

For each niche, score yourself 1-10 on:
1. **Knowledge fit** — how well do you already know this topic?
2. **Boredom tolerance** — can you spend 10 hours/week immersed in this?
3. **Production capacity** — can you handle the technical setup?
4. **Risk tolerance** — how OK are you with rejection/demonetization?

Multiply scores. The niche with the highest product wins.

## Niche 1: AI / Tech / Productivity

**Why it works in 2026:**
- Massive search demand (every business is trying to "use AI")
- High CPM ($12–$30) because the audience is corporate decision-makers
- You can leverage everything you're already learning
- Talking-head style works WITHOUT showing face (screen recording + B-roll)

**Why it might not:**
- Saturated. To win, you need a specific angle nobody else has
- Algorithm penalizes generic "ChatGPT tutorials"
- Burns out fast if you don't enjoy the topic

**Content example formats:**
- "I built X with Claude in 24 hours" (build-in-public)
- "The Y prompt that replaces $Z/hour" (replacement framing)
- "Top N tools for [specific job]" (listicle)
- "I tested 10 AI tools so you don't have to" (comparison)

**Workflow file:** `workflow_ai_tech.md`

## Niche 2: Finance / Investing

**Why it works:**
- Highest RPM of any niche ($15–$50)
- Evergreen demand (people will Google "how to invest" forever)
- Easy to monetize beyond AdSense (affiliate, courses, brokerage referrals)

**Why it might not:**
- Brutal competition (everyone wants this niche)
- Compliance risk (you must NOT give investment advice without disclaimers)
- Requires real domain knowledge or you'll be exposed fast
- YouTube finance algorithm is brutally strict on click-bait

**Content example formats:**
- "Why I sold $X of [stock]" (personal stake framing — even if hypothetical)
- "5 dividend stocks for [year]" (listicle, evergreen)
- "[Currency] is about to crash, here's why" (timely)
- "How [demographic] should invest $X" (target-specific)

**Workflow file:** `workflow_finance.md`

## Niche 3: Data / Rankings ($372K/mo model)

**Why it works:**
- The "Real Data" model: ranking countries by [thing], visualized with motion graphics
- LOW competition compared to AI or Finance
- High repeatability — once Remotion is set up, you can produce a video in 30 min
- Watch time multipliers are high (people watch the whole ranking)
- Channels in this niche regularly hit 400K–1M+ subscribers

**Why it might not:**
- Technical setup is real (Node.js, Git, Remotion, Claude Code, ElevenLabs, WaveSpeed)
- Lower RPM ($5–$15) so you need higher view volume
- Can feel "dry" — boredom risk if you don't like data

**Content example formats:**
- "Top 10 countries by [GDP / population / military / etc.]"
- "Every [city / country / year] ranked by [metric]"
- "What if [country] disappeared?" (counterfactual framing)
- "Comparison: [A] vs [B] across [N] dimensions"

**Workflow file:** `workflow_data_rankings.md`

## Niche 4: Kids (AI Animated)

**Why it works:**
- INSANE watch time multipliers (toddlers rewatch 5-10x)
- Algorithm aggressively pushes to YouTube Kids
- Some operators reporting $500/day to $23K/month
- Production is fast with Kling 3.0 / Runway

**Why it might not (the honest section):**
- **YouTube Kids policy is strict.** Many channels get banned or demonetized.
- COPPA compliance is non-negotiable (no behavioral ads, etc.)
- The "easy money" stories are survivor bias — most attempts die
- The audience (toddlers) can't engage, comment, or subscribe normally — discovery is 100% algorithmic
- If/when YouTube cracks down on AI kids content, your channel could vanish overnight

**Content example formats:**
- Animal characters singing nursery rhymes
- Counting / alphabet videos with characters
- Vehicle / dinosaur / shape animated stories
- "Try not to laugh" type compilations with original characters

**Workflow file:** `workflow_kids.md`

## My recommendation (if you can't decide)

**Default pick: AI / Tech / Productivity.** You're already in this ecosystem. You have an unfair information advantage. The RPM is high enough that even modest traction earns real money. Production complexity is low. Worst case, you build an audience that you can pivot to a different niche.

**Aggressive pick: Data / Rankings.** Highest ceiling. Lowest competition. Most fun if you like systems. Real path to $10K+/month within 6–9 months IF you ship consistently.

**Skip:** Kids — unless you're explicitly OK with platform risk and can absorb a banned channel.

## Decision is final
Once you pick, commit for 90 days. The biggest cause of failure is switching niches at video 8 because traction is slow. Trust the timeline. Build the system.
