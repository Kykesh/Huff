# Folder Structure — Why Each Folder Exists

The structure is intentionally minimal. 5 top-level folders. Each one serves exactly one job. Read once. Don't change it.

## 00-INBOX/
**Purpose:** Raw, unprocessed captures land here from any source.

**What goes in:**
- Voice memo transcriptions
- Things you noticed in passing
- Tweets you bookmarked
- Numbers you saw
- Questions that hit you
- Quick reactions

**Rule:** When in doubt, INBOX it. Don't agonize over where it belongs.

**Cleared by:** The "process my inbox" skill, daily.

---

## 01-CAPTURES/
**Purpose:** Processed notes, filed by TYPE, not topic.

This is the most important design decision. Most knowledge systems organize by topic (AI, business, fitness). That kills connection-finding because two notes on the same principle in different topics never meet.

By organizing by TYPE, "AI content strategy" and "how attention works psychologically" both land in `patterns/`. Claude finds the connection automatically.

### observations/
Things you NOTICED. Factual, neutral.
Example: "Stripe's checkout converts 30% better than PayPal in our A/B test."

### reactions/
Your GUT response to something. Subjective, emotional.
Example: "Felt frustrated reading the Notion docs — too much marketing fluff before the actual answer."

### patterns/
The SAME PRINCIPLE showing up in two different domains.
Example: "Open loops drive YouTube retention AND sales call retention AND song writing retention. Same mechanism: anticipation."

### questions/
Things you GENUINELY DON'T KNOW. Save the question even if you don't have time to answer it.
Example: "Why do faceless YouTube channels in the data niche grow faster than in tech?"

### numbers/
REAL DATA POINTS with specific numbers. Build a citable evidence base.
Example: "ElevenLabs Adam voice has 40% of public AI voiceover share in 2026 per industry survey."

---

## 02-CONNECTIONS/
**Purpose:** Synthesized insights from 2 OR MORE captured notes.

This folder is NEVER manually populated. It's only generated by the "run connection session" skill, which surfaces non-obvious connections across captures.

A "strong connection" is one of:
- Type A: Same principle in two domains
- Type B: Contradiction between two notes creating tension
- Type C: Pattern across 3+ notes pointing to an unnamed insight
- Type D: Question from one note that another note answers

---

## 03-BRIEFS/
**Purpose:** Content briefs ready to be written.

A brief is the 5-field format:
1. ONE THING (the single insight)
2. PROOF (the specific number/example)
3. READER TRANSFORMATION (what they know at the end)
4. THREE HOOKS (ranked)
5. THREE CLOSERS (ranked)

A brief takes a connection + adds the packaging needed to turn it into a Twitter thread, blog post, YouTube script, or LinkedIn post.

---

## 04-PUBLISHED/
**Purpose:** Archive of shipped content with performance data appended.

When you publish anything, archive the final draft here with metadata:
- Where published
- Date
- Views / likes / comments / DMs after 30 days
- What you'd change

Over time this folder becomes a TRAINING SET for what works in YOUR voice. Claude can study it to write more like you, faster.

---

## 05-CLAUDE/
**Purpose:** Everything about how Claude should operate.

Contains:
- `CLAUDE.md` (already in root, but referenced here)
- `skills/` — the 4 reusable workflows

Don't add anything else to this folder unless it's a Claude operational file.

---

## The architectural rules

1. **No folder for "later"** — that's what `00-INBOX/` is.
2. **No project folders** — projects come and go; types persist.
3. **No date folders** — files are timestamped in their names if needed.
4. **No "drafts" folder outside of `03-BRIEFS/`** — anything that's not a finished published piece is either a capture or a brief.
5. **Subfolders forbidden except where defined here** — every new subfolder fragments connection-finding.

If you find yourself wanting to add a folder, that's a sign you're trying to escape the discipline of the system. Don't.
