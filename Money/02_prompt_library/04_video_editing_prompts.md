# Video Editing Prompts

## 1. The Editing Blueprint
```
Act as a senior video editor with 15+ years of experience in YouTube and documentary editing. I will provide a description of raw footage — analyze it and create a clear, chronological editing blueprint that transforms it into a polished, high-retention video.

Deliverables:
- shot list in order
- cut points with rationale
- pacing notes (where to slow down, speed up)
- transitions (only where they add value)
- B-roll insertion points
- sound design / music cue placements
- text overlay moments

Raw footage description: [DESCRIBE]
Target length: [LENGTH]
Platform: [PLATFORM]
```

## 2. Style Reverse-Engineering
```
Act as a master video editor and visual style analyst. Analyze the reference video I provide and break down its core editing elements:
- pacing (avg shot length)
- transition types
- shot duration patterns
- color grading
- sound design
- background music style
- on-screen text usage
- camera movement

Then give me a replicable formula I can apply to my own content.

Reference video: [LINK or DESCRIPTION]
My content: [DESCRIBE]
```

## 3. Script Flow Optimization
```
Act as a content retention strategist specializing in YouTube and short-form videos. Review this script and analyze pacing, clarity, and engagement.

Identify weak points where:
- energy drops
- repetition occurs
- viewer attention may fade
- the hook fails to deliver
- the CTA loses momentum

Rewrite each weak section with:
- pattern interrupt
- escalation
- a specific image or number
- tighter language

Script: [PASTE]
```

## 4. Technical Export Setup
```
Act as a post-production supervisor responsible for broadcast-quality delivery. Based on the platform and content style I provide, recommend the optimal export settings to preserve maximum visual and audio quality while minimizing compression issues.

Provide:
- resolution
- frame rate
- bitrate
- codec
- color space
- audio: sample rate, bitrate, channels
- file format

Platform: [YOUTUBE / TIKTOK / IG / etc.]
Content style: [DESCRIBE]
```

## 5. Raw Footage Triage
```
Act as a lead video editor reviewing raw footage for a professional production. Analyze the uploaded footage and identify the most valuable moments. Organize into 3 categories:

GOLD — strong takes, key moments, must-include
SILVER — usable B-roll, transition fodder, context
DELETE — flubs, dead air, unusable

For each GOLD moment: timestamp + why it's gold + where in the final video it belongs.

Footage description: [DESCRIBE]
```

## 6. Final Polish Review
```
Act as a creative director reviewing a nearly finished video before publication. Evaluate the video from a first-time viewer's perspective and score it out of 10 across:
- pacing
- audio balance
- visual clarity
- color grading
- storytelling
- hook strength
- CTA clarity

For each score below 8: specific fix with exact timestamp and 1-sentence description.

Video description: [DESCRIBE]
```

## 7. Retention Audit
```
Act as an audience retention analyst specializing in online video performance. Review the provided video or script and identify where viewers are most likely to lose interest or drop off.

Pinpoint specific timestamps. For each:
- what weakens engagement at this moment
- targeted fix (pattern interrupt, tighter cut, B-roll insert, pacing change)

Also create 3 alternative high-energy hooks for the first 10 seconds designed to maximize curiosity and retention.

Upload video or paste script: [INSERT]
```
