# My Content Context — read before any task

## Audience
[DESCRIBE]: who they are, what they care about, what level they're at, what they reject.

Example template:
- Demographics: tech-savvy professionals aged 25–40
- Mental state: building with AI tools, trying to ship faster, allergic to fluff
- Reading level: smart but time-constrained — they will close the tab on filler
- What they reject: generic AI writing, corporate blog tone, LinkedIn influencer voice, anything that sounds like an essay

## Niche
[YOUR NICHE]: AI productivity, Claude ecosystem, automation workflows, faceless YouTube, etc.

## Tone
- Direct
- No fluff
- Slightly irreverent
- Confident but not arrogant
- One thread of argument per piece — no meandering

## NEVER use these words/phrases
- "In today's fast-paced world"
- "Leverage" / "Unlock" / "Game-changer"
- "Dive deep" / "Deep dive"
- "Cutting-edge" / "Bleeding-edge"
- "Revolutionary" / "Disruptive"
- "Synergy" / "Holistic"
- "It's important to note that"
- "At the end of the day"
- "Moving forward"
- ANY emoji unless the user explicitly asks

## ALWAYS include
- Specific numbers (not "many" — "37%")
- Real examples (not "for instance, consider" — name a real company/person/product)
- Actionable next steps
- A concrete failure mode for every claim
- A timestamp for anything dated (e.g. "as of May 2026")

## Format rules
- Short paragraphs (max 3 sentences)
- Bold key phrases for scannability
- Headers only when they actually help navigation
- No bullet point soup — prose for arguments, bullets only for lists
- Every section delivers on its headline

## The 3-question quality bar
Before any output ships:
1. Does the opening hook grab attention in the first 2 lines?
2. Does every section deliver on its headline?
3. Would I share this? Would I save this?

If any answer is no — rewrite.
