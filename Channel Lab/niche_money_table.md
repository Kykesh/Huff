# Niche Money Table — RPM Per Subject

The "money amounts per subject" reference, rebuilt and expanded from `Money/01_notes/01_youtube_automation.md` (7 niches) to 14, verified against 2026 industry data. Last verified: **June 2026** — `/scout` re-checks a niche's numbers every time it runs.

**Reading the numbers:** RPM = what YOU keep per 1,000 views (after YouTube's ~45% cut, including non-monetized views). CPM = what advertisers pay; blogs quote it because it's bigger. Finance "CPM $15–50" ≈ RPM $7–25. This table is RPM.

**The equation that matters:** Revenue = RPM × view scale. A $6-RPM niche pulling 2M views/mo beats a $25-RPM niche pulling 300K. High-RPM niches are high-RPM because advertisers fight for buyers — which means creator competition is brutal too.

## What the table says about channel #2

The cheapest expansions are the ones where automation fit ≥ 8 — **Geography/Maps, Luxury/Net-Worth, History/Documentary, Space** — because they rerun the Ranked Reality pipeline with new skins instead of funding a new build. Luxury/net-worth is effectively Data/Rankings with a higher-CPM advertiser pool. History has the best watch-time economics in the list. Don't pick from this table directly — `/scout` the shortlist when the portfolio guardrail lifts; numbers and competition shift quarterly.
